👾 Space Invaders

🏠

How to Play

Goal: Destroy all the alien invaders before they reach the bottom.

Move
Arrow keys or drag on the screen to move your ship left/right.
Shoot
Press Space or tap the screen to fire. One shot on screen at a time.
Invaders
Aliens march sideways and drop down when they hit an edge. They speed up as you destroy them.
Scoring
Top row = 30 pts, middle rows = 20 pts, bottom rows = 10 pts.
Lives
You have 3 lives. Getting hit by an alien bullet costs one life.
