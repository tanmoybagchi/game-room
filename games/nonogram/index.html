How to Play

Goal: Fill in cells to reveal a hidden pattern using the number clues.

Clues
Numbers beside each row and above each column tell you how many consecutive filled cells appear, in order. Groups are separated by at least one empty cell.
Fill
Tap a cell to fill it.
Mark Empty
Toggle ✖ mode then tap, or right-click, to mark a cell as definitely empty.
Undo
Tap a filled or marked cell again to clear it.
