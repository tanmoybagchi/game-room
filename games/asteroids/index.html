How to Play

Goal: Destroy all asteroids without getting hit.

Rotate
Left / Right arrow keys, or drag left/right on screen.
Thrust
Up arrow key, or tap the left side of the screen.
Shoot
Space bar, or tap the right side of the screen.
Asteroids
Large asteroids split into medium, medium into small. Small ones are destroyed.
Scoring
Large = 20 pts, Medium = 50 pts, Small = 100 pts.
Lives
You have 3 lives. Colliding with an asteroid costs one.
