Tower Defense

🏠

How to Play

Goal: Defend your base by building towers to destroy alien waves.

Place Towers
Select a tower from the bottom panel, then tap any open tile (not on the path) to place it.
Upgrade / Sell
Tap a placed tower to see upgrade and sell options.
Waves
Survive all 10 waves to win. Press "Next Wave" when ready between waves.
Lives
You start with 20 lives. Enemies that reach the exit cost lives.
Credits
Earn credits by killing enemies and completing waves. Spend them on towers and upgrades.
