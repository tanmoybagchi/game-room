How to Play

Goal: Break all the bricks by bouncing the ball off the paddle.

Move Paddle
Drag on the canvas or use left/right arrow keys.
Launch Ball
Tap the canvas or press Space to launch.
Lives
You have 3 lives. Losing the ball costs one life.
Scoring
Each brick is worth points based on its row. Higher rows score more.
