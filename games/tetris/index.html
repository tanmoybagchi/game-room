How to Play

Goal: Clear lines by filling complete rows with blocks.

Move
Swipe left/right or use arrow keys to move the piece.
Rotate
Tap the board or press Up arrow to rotate.
Soft Drop
Swipe down or press Down arrow to drop faster.
Hard Drop
Swipe up or press Space to instantly drop the piece.
Scoring
1 line = 100 pts, 2 = 300, 3 = 500, 4 (Tetris) = 800. Level multiplier applies.
